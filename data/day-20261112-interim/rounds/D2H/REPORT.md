# D2H: DtoH per step in n=2 decode (CPU-only trace analysis)
Verdict: the "6 DtoH/step x 110 us" is NOT in the traces; steady decode has 1 big DtoH/step (+1 tiny in D1BF). Bursts of many DtoH are state/checkpoint dumps.
- D1BF ks_p1/ks_p0 (tool dx2seq, pair, 40 steps): per decode step exactly 2 DtoH: [1.894531 MiB = 2 x 248320 x 4 B logits, 0.152 ms, after mul_mat_vec_q/head] + [8 B, 2 us, after ggml_cuda_hg_gather]. 0.947 MiB x4 = prefill-end logits (head_rowpack_sm52, 0.078 ms).
- Tool-only DtoH: after the last step, state_seq_get_data dumps: 2.97 MiB x20, 96 KiB x40, 2 MiB x60-ish (0.16 ms each). Tool also does llama_get_logits_ith + fwrite (host side, no extra DtoH). These look like "many 100-160 us calls" but are post-decode, not per step.
- D0 deep.csv (prod llama-server, 98K+90K): 884 DtoH = bursts of 30 x (96 KiB + 2 MiB, 0.16 ms) at 7 time points (checkpoint/slot-state saves, 2 per ~100 s, not per step) + 2x 20 x ~90 MB (ctx checkpoint) + logits: 46 x 1.85 MiB (2-seq logits, 0.153 ms, once per step) + 1-24 x 0.925 MiB at prefill ends (head_rowpack, 0.078 ms). So server = 1 logits DtoH/step (46 steps = 46 copies, not 5/step; the 231 count includes the bursts).
- The "~5-6/step" most likely = those 30-copy bursts divided by few steps, or a window that includes state dumps. The 110 us mean fits the 96 KiB/2 MiB dump mix (9 us / 162 us), not a per-step pattern.
Server-path copies per step: (1) full logits both slots, 1.89 MiB, 0.152 ms: required only because common_sampler runs on CPU; (2) 8 B hg_gather result, 2 us, needed.
Avoidable: logits copy could be replaced by backend sampling (ids only; code exists: llama_context get_sampled_*, tools/server server-context.cpp:1930 params.sampling.backend_sampling, auto-disabled when pre-sample logits are needed, e.g. logprobs/grammar). Saves ~0.15 ms/step (plus the sync it implies) minus a few us for ids = ~0.14-0.15 ms/step (~0.4% of 40 ms pair step). Below the 0.4 cap; low priority.
Not avoidable/needed: state/checkpoint dumps (off the step path; they cost ~0.16 ms x 30 stalls per burst, only at checkpoint time).
Caveat: confirm with `backend_sampling` default and request params in the D0 run (not checked); nothing run on GPU.
