LIMITS / NEXT DECISION

One fixture, 64 labels and four full distributions remain a bounded screen.
Its multiple checkpoint requests are not a single-request TTFT benchmark.
The 98K hex/digit-heavy region is particularly low entropy; do not let its
small KL obscure the 65K distribution movement. The separate deterministic
controls establish that the earlier 16K difference is reproducible, not run
noise or tested baseline 512-versus-1024 microbatch sensitivity. This grid
uses a different checkpoint profile and trajectory from that control.
Root's semantic retrieval receipts must be reported separately; passing
retrievals do not erase this negative numerical evidence.
