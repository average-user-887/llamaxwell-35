PACKED-GQA FP32 GEMM ATTENTION V2
Built and frozen 2026-11-12 14:41:22 UTC. No GPU use by this worker.

Change from v1
The eight query heads sharing each KV head are packed into one contiguous
matrix per query tile. Default 64-query tile becomes 512 GEMM columns.
QK is one SGEMM per group rather than eight skinny batched SGEMMs.
PV uses 24 split GEMMs with output shape256x512, rather than192 GEMMs256x64.
This preserves exactly the same score and partial-product storage sizes.
Softmax implementation, query shape, precision, masks, safety thresholds,
and ABBA methodology are unchanged from v1, allowing attribution.

Packing is performed once per timed candidate call and reported as packing_ms.
Conversion, packing, QK, softmax, PV, reduction, and submission gaps are all
included in milliseconds. Setup/allocation/input transfer remain excluded.
Steady-state-only kernel timings are not end-to-end serving claims.

Actual shape and layout
16Q heads,2KV heads,D256, full causal context.
Packed Q layout: [query_tile,KV_group,local_head,local_query,dimension].
Scores: [local_head,local_query,key], as in v1.
Partials: [split,local_head,local_query,dimension].
Reduction output: [global_query,global_head,dimension], as in reference.
CPU checks proved packing is bijective for64/128/1024queries and32/64tiles,
and checked split-product addressing for2/24/32splits and boundary dimensions.
The upcoming GPU comparison remains the numerical acceptance evidence.
