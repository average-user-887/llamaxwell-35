Strong source-backed cause: dense Q4 output-head dequantization cliff
MMVQ_MAX_BATCH_SIZE is8 (mmvq.cuh:3); SM52's generic quantized selector permits
up to8 columns (mmvq.cu:430). Eight drafted tokens plus the anchor require up
to9 verification/logit columns, unlike normal one-token decode or last-token-
only prompt output. Maxwell MMQ deliberately admits only n_experts>0
(mmq.cu:413); the dense output projection therefore falls through to cuBLAS.
SM52 selects F32 compute for quantized source (ggml-cuda.cu:1806).
The existing512MiB chunked-conversion guard covers F16/BF16 only, not Q4_0
(ggml-cuda.cu:1843), so Q4 source is converted whole.
