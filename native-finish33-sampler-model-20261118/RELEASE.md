The exact original server-implementation relink matches the source parent, and the candidate retains its dynamic symbols and loader metadata.

The device-free clone screen calls the pinned runtime's actual shared-library clone and free on a synthetic 248,320-entry sampler with its full 2,979,840-byte token vector. Across three independent processes, mean clone plus free was 52.47–54.87 microseconds with warm cache and 196.40–200.02 microseconds after each untimed 64 MiB sweep. The real model's cache residency is unknown. The empty-vector control was about 0.67 microseconds. See `clone-screen.log` for all three process means and quantiles.

The combined runner uses exactly the retained-attention DSO whose complete operator gained 27.18 and 26.72 microseconds in mirrored physical 81,920-key runs at the 2,048-byte pitch. It preserved exact outputs and missed its standalone 35-microsecond gate. The 1,024-byte pitch was independently exact in diagnostic replay. The 0.2672 ms saving across ten attention layers is a projection. Together with the warm CPU result it projects roughly 0.320 ms against the measured 0.32775 ms gap, so the combined result requires physical measurement. Neither standalone screen establishes whole-model performance.
